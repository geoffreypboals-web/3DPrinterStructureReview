`library/duender_generated.json` contains measurements (section areas, inertias, bounding boxes) and parts
lists taken from printer CAD in that project. That CAD builds on Duender (GPL-2.0) and
Andizzle's triple-Z parts. The print settings in `library/base_library.json` follow the Duender manuals. No CAD
geometry from those projects is included here, only numbers derived from it. Those projects keep their own licences.
